# The Angkor Times

A newsroom. Editors prepare news and categories. Admins decide what is published and who belongs.

## Language

**News**:
A piece of reporting prepared by an Editor. It becomes public only when an Admin publishes it.
_Avoid_: story, article, post

**Pending**:
News an Editor has prepared that an Admin has not published.
_Avoid_: draft, unpublished

**Published**:
News an Admin has made public.
_Avoid_: live, posted

**Author**:
The Editor who created a piece of news. The name on the piece stays theirs when another Editor revises it.
_Avoid_: last editor, byline

**Category**:
A section that news belongs to.
_Avoid_: tag, topic

**Editor**:
A newsroom member who prepares news and categories. Editors may be humans or agents, and the newsroom may have more than one of each.
_Avoid_: Admin

**Agent**:
An Editor that is not a human. An agent holds the Editor role, not a role of its own.
_Avoid_: a third role, Admin

**Admin**:
A newsroom member who decides what is published and who belongs to the newsroom.
_Avoid_: Editor

**Hermes**:
One agent Editor. Other Editors, human or agent, may work alongside it.
_Avoid_: the only Editor, Admin, chatbot
